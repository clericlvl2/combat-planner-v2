## Questions

1. SortSheet: does a pick close the sheet? The brief (criterion 3) says choosing an option calls `onChange(value)` and closes; `docs/design/handoff/SortSheet.dc.html` Flow ("The pick applies at once; Done closes", cell "A–Z selected" with the sheet still open) and `RadioList.dc.html` overview say the sheet stays open until Done. The screen flows `CombatList.dc.html` CL-F1.3 → CL-F1.4 and `Library.dc.html` LB-F2.1 → LB-F2.2 show the sheet closed after the pick. Built per the brief: a click (or Enter/Space on an unchecked row) calls `onChange(value)` then `onClose()`. Arrow keys move the native radio selection and apply it through `onChange` but keep the sheet open (`SortSheet.tsx`, `movedByArrow`), because closing on every arrow press would break the ARIA radiogroup keyboard path; Done, ×, Escape and the scrim close via `onClose`. A tap on the already selected row fires nothing (RadioList does not emit an unchanged value) and does not close.
