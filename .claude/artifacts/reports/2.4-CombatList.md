## Questions

1. `CombatSummary` (`packages/api/src/client.ts:60`) has no `round`, so the card meta of an Active combat reads `6 units` instead of `Round 3 · 6 units`. `CombatList` passes `round={null}` to `CombatCard`. The summary needs a `round` field (API and `FakeApiClient.summaryOf`, both outside this batch).
2. `CombatSummary` has no `createdAt`. Newest first keeps the order of `combats.list`, which sorts by `createdAt` descending in `FakeApiClient`; the real server must keep that order.
3. The brief places `CombatFormSheet` at `packages/ui/src/screens/CombatList/CombatFormSheet.tsx`, but `scripts/check-commit.sh` (lines 53–57) rejects a commit that adds `__screenshots__/CombatFormSheet/` without a change under `src/**/CombatFormSheet/`. The component lives in `packages/ui/src/screens/CombatList/CombatFormSheet/` (`CombatFormSheet.tsx`, `.module.css`, `.stories.tsx`), inside the allowed `screens/CombatList/**`. The two commits were rebuilt with `git reset --soft` before the hand-back to make this move; no rebase.
4. Brief: Create is disabled until the title is valid; render CL-F2.3 shows the error after tapping Create. A disabled button cannot be tapped, so the «Enter a title» error appears when the title field loses focus while empty (CL-F2.3 play: focus Title, Tab). The renders draw Create in the enabled colour in CL-F2.2 and CL-F2.3.
5. `Sheet` (`packages/ui/src/organisms/Sheet/Sheet.tsx:35-39`) moves focus to the sheet itself in a `setTimeout`, so a form field cannot be autofocused on open; CL-F2.2 shows the title without a caret. The `Sheet` content also runs Mantine's 200 ms transition with `motion: 'off'` (visible as `transition-duration: 200ms`, `opacity: 0` right after open); the stories wait for opacity 1 before asserting.
6. Story access to the fake client: `withApi.tsx` exports `apiLoader` (registered as `loaders: [apiLoader]` in `preview.tsx`, next to the `withApi` decorator) and `fakeApiOf(loaded)`. The loader builds `FakeApiClient({ scenario, delay: 0 })` and a fresh `QueryClient` per story run; the decorator wraps the story in `ApiProvider` with them; `play` reads `calls` through `fakeApiOf(loaded)`. A decorator alone has no channel to hand the client to `play`.
7. Navigation assertions: the stories route `*` to the story element and render a hidden `LocationProbe` (`data-location`), so after Create the list stays on screen (matching render CL-F2.5) while the probe proves the move to `/combats/<new id>`.
8. Search covers title and description. Render CL-F1.5 shows «Капитан стражи переправы…» and «黒水の渡し守» as hits for “ferry”, which their page titles do not contain; the scenario gives them descriptions containing “Ferry”/“ferryman” (`CombatList.scenarios.ts`).
9. `CombatCard` has no weeks wording; a combat played 14 days ago reads `14 days ago` where render CL-F1.4 shows `2 weeks ago`.
10. Toast icons: `useToasts` items carry no kind, so `CombatList` remembers the message it expects for Duplicate (`IconCopy`, success) and Delete (`IconTrash`, neutral); a toast with an action gets `IconWifiOff` (error), any other `IconAlertTriangle` (warning).
