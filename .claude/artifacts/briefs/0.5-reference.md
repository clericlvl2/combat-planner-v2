## Story naming

Export name = PascalCase of `<row label> <column header>` with every
non-alphanumeric character dropped: `Primary L + icon` × `default` →
`PrimaryLIconDefault`; `Button · primary` × `S` → `ButtonPrimaryS`; `Primary M` ×
`full width` → `PrimaryMFullWidth`; the Anatomy row → `Anatomy`. Extra stories carry
the prefix `Extra` (`ExtraKeyboard`). A story's `name` may hold the human label.
