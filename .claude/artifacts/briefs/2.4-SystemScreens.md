## Acceptance criteria

1. `COMBAT_PLANNER_NO_DOCKER=1 pnpm check`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm storybook:build`
   exit 0; `pnpm exec vp test --project visual -t <Name>` passes for every
   screen without `--update`.
2. Each screen renders the icon, title, body and action of its row above,
   matching `AppPages-AP-F5.png` and `MessageScreen.png`; every story has a
   390-wide baseline.
3. Screens with an action fire their callback once on click and on Enter;
   `LinkExpired` and `CombatDeleted` render no button; `CombatDeleted` shows
   the passed title.
4. Six i18n files per new namespace with identical keys; `check-i18n` passes;
   no string from `MessageScreen.json` is duplicated.
5. One commit `feat(ui): <Name>` per screen, each inside its paths; no
   comments.
