  `apiLoader` (registered in `preview.tsx`; one `FakeApiClient` and

  `__screenshots__/<Component>/` against `src/**/<Component>/`).

  where a render draws it enabled; the error shows when the field loses focus
  while empty. Listed under Differences.

## Units, in this order, one commit each

Each screen in `packages/ui/src/screens/<Name>/` with `<Name>.tsx`,
`<Name>.module.css`, `<Name>.stories.tsx` (no page of its own: free story
names, `Default` plus the states the unit lists), i18n namespace `<Name>`.

1. `SignIn`: email, password, Sign in (disabled until both are filled);
   «Wrong email or password» on `unauthorized`; social buttons only for the
   providers `useAccount().providers` returns (none → no social block,
   AP-F1.7); a social button calls `socialSignIn(provider, callbackUrl)` and
   assigns the returned URL to `window.location` through an `onRedirect`
   prop (default `window.location.assign`) so stories assert it; link to
   Sign up; success navigates to `/combats`; the toast «Your account was
   deleted» when routed with `state.accountDeleted` (AP-F3.5).
2. `SignUp`: display name (`LIMITS.displayName`), email, password; errors
   «At least 8 characters» and «This email already has an account»
   (AP-F1.5); success navigates to an empty `/combats`.
3. `Settings`: `PageHeader` with burger; `RadioList` of the six locales with
   endonym labels from `packages/ui/src/screens/Settings/localeNames.ts`
   (English, Deutsch, Español, Français, 日本語, Русский — not translated);
   a choice calls `useSettings().update` and the interface switches at once
   (AP-F2.3).
4. `Account`: email, display name edit (`update`), Sign out (lands on
   `/auth/sign-in`), Delete account with `Dialog` confirm, then `remove` and
   navigate to `/auth/sign-in` with `state.accountDeleted`.
5. `About`: rows per the plan list above; version from a `version` prop
   (4-web passes the build version); Source, Donate links from props
   `sourceUrl`, `donateUrl`; Rules «13th Age»; English copy for the
   description, privacy note and credits written by the agent and translated
   into five locales.
6. `AppPages` (`feat(ui): AppPages`): `packages/ui/src/screens/AppPages/AppPages.stories.tsx`
   with every `AP-F*` step story the script requires, composing units 1–5,
   the `Drawer` (AP-F2.1) and the system screens (AP-F5.1–F5.3), each seeded
   through `scenario` and routed through `withRouter`.
